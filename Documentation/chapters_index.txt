PROJECT SUMMARY
---------------
This repository is structured as a practical learning progression in WebGL fundamentals, starting from the first triangle and progressing through geometry, topology, shader data flow, screen-space math, and matrix transforms.

Notable repository fact:
- The project has expanded beyond the original chapter sequence, with active lesson files in chapter07 and chapter08, while chapter11, chapter12, and chapter21 remain placeholders for future work.
